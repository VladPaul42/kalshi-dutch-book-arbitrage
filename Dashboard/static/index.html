Fee assumptions

Every edge figure is an estimate built on these assumptions, not a quote. The same taker model (M=1, top of book) applies to all four basket routes. Kalshi sets series-specific multipliers and charges maker fees on some series; neither is modelled.

Score

score = max(volume_24h_contracts)

The score is the busiest leg's 24-hour volume, in contracts. A missing or negative volume on any leg keeps the event out of the ranking. Best edge and best route are labels beside the rank.
